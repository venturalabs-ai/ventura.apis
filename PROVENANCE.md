# Provenance

## Scope
`ventura.apis` is an independent curation and workflow repository maintained by Ventura Labs AI.

## External references
Community catalogs such as `public-apis` may be used as discovery references. Their source files, curated lists, descriptions, ordering and other protected material must not be copied, translated, redistributed or relicensed here unless the applicable license terms are verified and preserved.

## Contribution rule
Any third-party material must record: source project, exact URL, license/terms, date checked, material reused, modifications and required notices. If provenance is unclear, do not merge it.

## Licensing boundary
The repository license applies only to content that Ventura Labs AI has the right to license. APIs and third-party documentation remain governed by their own terms.
